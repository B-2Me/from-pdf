### FOREWORD BY JOHN COBB, JR.

The literature on Whitehead is growing and improving. Matt Segall's work is at the cutting edge.

Most of the earlier work was a struggle to understand and explain the cosmology and metaphysics at which Whitehead arrived so as to present a systematic statement of his thought that can be put into dialogue with other systems of thought. This has been accompanied by application of his vision in and beyond the fields he treated. Others wrote introductions, and there are books available for people with diverse needs and interests. All this is important. But the tendency has been to explain "process" systematically without explaining the process in which these ideas arose and developed before Whitehead and, then, in his own thinking.

I have myself become increasingly aware of the remarkable process of Whitehead's thinking that went on in the course of his writing *Process and Reality*. If Whitehead had had in mind, at the outset, all the ideas with which he completed the book, we would have a different book. For example, still today most of us identify just two basic types of prehensions that are interwoven into many other types: physical feelings and conceptual feelings. But Whitehead discovered he also needed "strain feelings," and he introduced them with no revision of earlier statements. Also near the end of the writing, he discovered hybrid feelings and recognized their importance. The book would have been different if he had had this type of feeling in mind when writing all of it. Our understanding of the book would be different if we treated it as giving insight into the fresh and creative ideas that his work brought forth. Properly understood *Process and*  xii | MATTHEW DAVID SEGALL

*Reality* calls on those of us who have profited so much from Whitehead's thinking to continue the process he carried so far.

It is not Segall's task in this book to talk about the process of Whitehead's thinking, testified to within the writing of *Process and Reality*. But he prepares the ground for such work. He sets the whole of Whitehead's corpus within a process that began before Whitehead, and he shows how Whitehead's overall thinking developed in the course of his life. Whitehead prophetically recognized what is finally starting to be acknowledged by physicists today, that post-classical scientific cosmology has passed into an epicyclic phase of theoretical development.32 This has been the case for at least a century, as two of the most mathematically beautiful and empirically predictive theories yet articulated by science, quantum theory and relativity theory, still cannot be reconciled with one another and thus appear to offer two incompatible perspectives on the ultimate nature of the universe.
